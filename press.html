Long bio (150 words)

Mr Sayrws (mrsayrws on GitHub) is a reverse engineer and malware analyst focused on how software fails at the binary level. His work spans unpacking and analysing malware, tracing command-and-control infrastructure, building ROP chains, and memory and disk forensics. He is a CTF player and the author of open tools including StopStealer (a real-time Windows infostealer blocker), GOLEM-TABLET (Android diagnostics for Windows), Ad-Blocker (rootless DNS filtering on Android) and App2FA (an offline TOTP vault). He documents his process in his writing at mrsayrws.github.io and on YouTube at Mr Sayrws, where he publishes malware-analysis walkthroughs, reverse-engineering sessions and CTF solutions. He works only on systems he owns, CTFs, or engagements with written authorisation.
